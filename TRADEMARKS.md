If you distribute a build without that permission, choose a distinct product name and replace the original branding where it identifies the build, including in the app, installer, icons and download listing.

Use the marks in a way that avoids implying endorsement, affiliation or an official release. Keep copyright and license notices required by the applicable software licenses.
